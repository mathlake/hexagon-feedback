# Code of conduct

MathLake is committed to a constructive, rigorous, and welcoming forum.

Be respectful; critique ideas rather than people; give enough context for others
to understand a report; and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam,
doxxing, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. To report conduct privately, email
admin@mathlake.org. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
